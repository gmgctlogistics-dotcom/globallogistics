Corporate Profile

Dedicated Exclusively to Vehicle Logistics

Unlike general packers and movers who co-load cars with home furniture and industrial cargo, GLOBAL LOGISTICS was engineered from ground up as an automobile-only logistics network. Our fleet of 550+ double-decker open auto carriers and single/dual enclosed container haulers are built solely for passenger cars, luxury automobiles, and premium two-wheelers.

With over 18 years of operational excellence, we serve individual car owners, corporate relocation programs, armed forces personnel, automotive manufacturers, and multi-brand dealerships nationwide.

Our Philosophy

The Values That Drive Us Forward

Our commitment to safety, integrity, and transparency defines every vehicle we move.

1. Vehicle Safety First

We treat every car as a prized family possession. From the 54-point pre-transit condition audit to hydraulic deck wheel-locking, safety is never compromised for speed.

2. Transparent Quotations

No hidden transit surcharges, no unexpected octroi or toll additions at destination. What we quote includes doorstep pickup, transit insurance, and delivery handover.

3. Punctual Delivery Commitment

With dedicated dual-driver long-haul shifts and optimized highway routes, over 99.4% of all consignments are handed over on or before the committed delivery date.
