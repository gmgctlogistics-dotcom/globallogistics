Dedicated Exclusively to Vehicle Logistics

Unlike general packers and movers who co-load cars with home furniture and industrial cargo, GLOBAL LOGISTICS was engineered from ground up as an automobile-only logistics network. Our fleet of 550+ double-decker open auto carriers and single/dual enclosed container haulers are built solely for passenger cars, luxury automobiles, and premium two-wheelers.

With over 18 years of operational excellence, we serve individual car owners, corporate relocation programs, armed forces personnel, automotive manufacturers, and multi-brand dealerships nationwide.
