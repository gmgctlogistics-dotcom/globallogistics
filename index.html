How GLOBAL LOGISTICS Relocates Your Vehicle

From doorstep digital inspection to safe key handover at your destination, our standardized protocol ensures complete peace of mind.

01

54-Point Digital Inspection

Our certified logistics officer visits your doorstep, records pre-existing conditions, checks odometer & fuel levels, and generates a digital condition audit on our cloud portal.

02

Hydraulic Ramp Loading

Your vehicle is driven onto specialized hydraulic ramps with zero incline scratches. Heavy-duty non-abrasive wheel chocks lock all four tires in place to eliminate movement.

03

Insured GPS Transit

The carrier embarks on national highways with live GPS monitoring. You receive scheduled WhatsApp notifications and can track exact location coordinates 24 hours a day.

04

Doorstep Porch Handover

The carrier reaches your new address. A joint re-inspection confirms zero damages against the origin checklist, after which keys, documents, and sign-offs are completed.

Clarification & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about documentation, insurance coverage, transit timelines, and our vehicle shifting process.

What documents are mandatory for transporting a car across India?

To comply with RTO regulations and national highway checkposts, you will need to provide: (1) Photocopy of the Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), (2) Valid Comprehensive Vehicle Insurance policy, (3) Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate, and (4) Government Photo ID of the vehicle owner (Aadhaar or PAN). Our officer inspects these during the doorstep pickup.

What is the difference between an Open Carrier and an Enclosed Carrier?

An Open Carrier is a heavy-duty multi-tier truck carrying 6 to 8 vehicles; it is the most economical and popular choice for everyday hatchbacks and sedans. An Enclosed Carrier is a sealed containerized truck that protects the vehicle completely from highway dust, gravel, and adverse weather conditions; it is highly recommended for luxury cars, vintage vehicles, sports cars, and new electric vehicles.

How does transit insurance work and what damages are covered?

All consignments shipped with GLOBAL LOGISTICS include comprehensive marine transit cargo insurance backed by leading insurers (such as National Insurance, Tata AIG, and ICICI Lombard). It protects against collisions, accidental rollover, overturning, fire, flood, theft, and transit perils. In the rare event of an incident, our dedicated claims desk facilitates immediate cashless surveys.

Can I leave personal belongings or household items inside the car?

You may keep standard vehicle accessories (such as floor mats, emergency toolkit, spare tire, and clean boot luggage up to 30-40 kg if securely packed below window height). However, hazardous liquids, combustible fuels, jewelry, cash, and high-value electronics must never be left inside the vehicle as cargo insurance does not cover loose personal possessions.

How can I track my car in real-time during interstate transit?

Once loaded, you will receive a unique Tracking Docket number (e.g. GL-80419) via SMS and WhatsApp. You can enter this code in the live tracking tool on our website at any time to see the carrier's real-time satellite coordinates, highway toll crossings, and estimated time of arrival (ETA). Our 24/7 dispatch control room is also reachable via phone.

How much fuel should remain in the car tank before handover?

We recommend keeping approximately one-fourth (1/4th) of a tank of fuel. This allows sufficient fuel for loading and unloading the vehicle onto hydraulic ramps, maneuvering into parking terminals, and final delivery to your doorstep, while reducing truck freight weight and fire hazards.
